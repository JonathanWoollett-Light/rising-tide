# MLT conquest plan - the Mirelurk Tribe (MLT), Old World Blues + Rising Tide
# The simplified ideal strategy: the operations a human MLT performs, in order, from 2275-01-01 until the major powers are beaten.
# It keeps balance and AI work in perspective. MLT's AI follows it broadly (CLAUDE.md > MLT's AI); the plan, the code and the AI
#   change together, and python check_plan_sync.py must pass (ids, AI coverage and the '# AI deviation:' lines at the end).
# After a spectator run of the AI, python ai_run_report.py compares the run with this plan (CLAUDE.md > MLT's AI > Telemetry).
# A paper plan built on 2026-09-15 from the game files, brought up to rounds 28b-28c on 2026-09-22 and corrected the same day for
#   OWB's decimation, non-core, compliance, water, armour, caps-upkeep and special-forces rules (story_rework/BALANCE_REVIEW.md,
#   section 4, and a review of that pass); not play-tested.
#   [F] = read from a file, [M] = modelled estimate, read as a range.
# Assumes the default game rules (caps on, allow_wargoals LIMITED, Oregon unifier NONE, refugees and decimation on Factory
#   Decimation-Only), historical AI focuses (the game setup's default, which decides how the invitations are answered: see Act III),
#   La Resistance owned and normal AI.
# Costs are read from the mod's script, the one source of truth (CLAUDE.md does not restate them); the ritual column is Call 60,
#   Grand Ritual 120, Walk 60 and Final Ritual 180 days [F].
# Day 0 is 2275-01-01; years have no leap days. One focus runs at a time, and a focus's cost is its length in days [F].
#
# Justifying war goals [F]
#   Under allow_wargoals LIMITED a player needs no claim, no world tension and pays nothing extra.
#   take_state costs 200 + 50 per state of the target and drains 0.2 PP a day while it runs.
#   Each other war or justification running at the same time makes the next one 1.5x longer.
#   A target that is already at war counts as a major (OWB) and costs half.
#   Vanilla uses the same 200/50 costs, so expect roughly 2-4 months for a target of 1-4 states [M].
#   Check once on day 0: hover Justify on TRL and CCW. If it reads much over 90 days, push every war below back by the difference.
#
# Why rush [F]
#   Troll Warren (TRL) can fight no one before day 133 (its Wizard decision), and that first war can only be on the Carcass Walkers (CCW).
#   If MLT already owns CCW's 166 and 437 by then, TRL gets only a claim on MLT, and its mid-tree stays locked
#   (it needs TRL itself to annex CCW or MDT).
#   Left alone, TRL takes CCW (~2275-07), then RBT (from ~2275-09), then MDT (from ~2276-06), and only then turns on MLT.
#   So: CCW by ~day 125, TRL by ~day 270, RBT before MDT can make it a puppet (~2276-05). Nobody guarantees or allies any of them [F].
#
# Numbers used [F]
#   A military factory makes 2.0 IC a day at the 40 % efficiency cap: ~2.45 after scavtool, ~2.7 after specialised industry.
#   A mirelurk battalion is 375 IC, 30 manpower and 150 days of training.
#   A Coral Host (4 mirelurk + 5 infantry) is 1,750 IC and 245 manpower.
#   Mirelurk equipment needs 3 water per factory (`_2` 4, the armour and king forms 5; Deep Ones 5, Star Spawn 8). Short of it, a
#   factory works 5 % slower per missing unit: water slows a line, never stops it. Resources gain 10 % per infrastructure level, so
#   MLT's own 36 raw water is ~43, ~47 from lurk_import_thunder_sticks; conquered land adds its own at the non-core share below,
#   and water can be built and bought (Water, in the notes). The Wet Market sells equipment at 25 IC per cap.
#   Mirelurk armour is 15 (`_2` and the king form 20, the armour form 25), and every tribal and super-mutant weapon in Oregon has
#   piercing 1-2. Mirelurk piercing is 7, and all of those units have armour 0.
#   The exceptions are TRL's one Woods Monsters division (4 behemoth, armour 20, piercing 36) and the Brotherhoods' power armour:
#   TCA's one Paladin division in Portland and WBH's four PA Companies (6 power armour on laser rifles and an anti-tank company
#   each) pierce ~20 from 2275 - through mirelurk `_1` and level with `_2`.
#   Division armour and piercing are 0.4 x the best battalion + 0.6 x the average, so ONE mirelurk (armour 15) gives a militia
#   division armour ~6.75: above every Oregon and tribal weapon and the Legion's 2275 line (1-3), level with its Prime Legionaries
#   (spec-ops kit on their rifles, ~6-7). An anti-tank company (piercing 30 / 45, +20 % from anti_tank_tech_1) lifts an NCR-style
#   division to piercing ~15-22 (~25 with the 2281 gun), which pierces that and the 8-mirelurk Tide Wall (15, 20 on `_2`), and
#   lifts a Tide Wall's own piercing from 7-8 to ~21 against a power-armour division's ~23-27. OWB's AI puts one in its default
#   infantry template, and the Legion has both guns open (its settler support tier), so by 2282 its line pierces ~15-25 too; from
#   the NCR on, real armour (20+) matters [F defines, M].
#   Every marketplace sells at ~0.04 caps per IC (1 cap = 25 IC), in lots of 250, one lot per seller per 40 days [F].
#   MLT's own caps income is ~+12 a quarter (flat, never multiplied). Since round 17 the Kingdom makes M'lyeh a trade node: ~3.4 caps
#   a quarter at level 0, ~17 at level 3, the one income The Wet Market's +20 % multiplies. The Drowned Hoard (100 caps per 1,000
#   people every 60 days) is the big source [F formulas, M amounts]. OWB takes the army's upkeep in caps each quarter,
#   (B + B squared / 2,500) / 26 (caps_update_military), B being the battalions weighted by type - creatures x0.8, militia x0.65,
#   support companies x0.85, power armour x1.25, the rest (our Deep Ones and Star Spawn among them) x1 - plus deployed planes x0.5
#   and the garrison's manpower need / 100 x0.85: ~18 for 550 battalions of militia and mirelurks (2279) and ~40 for 1,000
#   mostly creatures (2285) before the garrison, whose need on 300-400 non-core states under Forced Labor is unmeasured and may
#   match the army's [F formula, M].
#   Summoned divisions come fully equipped, with 20 % spare in the stockpile (32 Deep Ones, 16 Star Spawn) [F].
#   Conquest [F rules, M timing]. OWB's default refugee-and-decimation rule, Factory Decimation-Only, keeps every conquered
#   person. A state that changes controller loses round(L x 2p - 2.5) of its L arms factories and round(L x 2p - 3.5) of its
#   civilian ones, p being 0.23 x the old owner's nomadicity (0.75, 0.9 for a tribe) / MLT's appeal (0.6 + half its stability)
#   (exodus_effects.txt). At MLT's stability on this plan (~40-70 % at war until the Final Ritual, ~75-95 % after it) a state
#   loses one only from ~6-10 arms or ~8-13 civilian levels, and no state on the map starts above 7 arms or 9 civilian.
#   What holds conquered industry back is OWB's non-core penalty (non_core_controller): land held without a core gives half its
#   factories, resources and building slots. Forced Labor, the elites' occupation law, which MLT sets from its first conquest,
#   adds 10 % (the default, Caravan Guards, takes 20 %), and compliance gives most of the rest back: OWB adds 0.2 x compliance to
#   local factories and resources (compliance_effect), and its tiers 5 % at 40, 15 % at 60 and 20 % at 80 (compliance_modifiers).
#   So a non-core state gives ~60 % when taken, ~73 % at 40 % compliance, ~87 % at 60 %, ~96 % at 80 %, and all once cored [F].
#   Compliance grows 0.05 a day (half as much again at peace, a quarter with a claim) plus Forced Labor's 0.025, and decays by up
#   to 0.083 a day as it nears 100 %: at war it settles near 90 %, passing 40 % after ~2 years and 60 % after ~3.5 [F defines, M].
#   Coring another nation's core land asks 95 % compliance and under 20 % resistance (no political power on the Pacific coast
#   since the Kingdom, 30-300 PP inland), which Forced Labor may not reach at war without a claim: land held since 2275-78 gives
#   back most of its output uncored, and coring adds the last few per cent, the manpower and the garrison saved.
