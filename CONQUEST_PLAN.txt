# MLT conquest plan - the Mirelurk Tribe (MLT), Old World Blues + Rising Tide
# The simplified ideal strategy: the operations a human MLT performs, in order, from 2275-01-01 until the major powers are beaten.
# It keeps balance and AI work in perspective. MLT's AI follows it broadly (CLAUDE.md > MLT's AI); the plan, the code and the AI
#   change together, and python check_plan_sync.py must pass (ids, AI coverage and the '# AI deviation:' lines at the end).
# After a spectator run of the AI, python ai_run_report.py compares the run with this plan (CLAUDE.md > MLT's AI > Telemetry).
# A paper plan built on 2026-09-15 from the game files; not play-tested. [F] = read from a file, [M] = modelled estimate, read as a range.
# Assumes the default game rules (caps on, allow_wargoals LIMITED, Oregon unifier NONE), La Resistance owned and normal AI.
# Costs are read from the mod's script, the one source of truth (CLAUDE.md does not restate them); the ritual column is Call 60,
#   Grand Ritual 120, Walk 60 and Final Ritual 180 days [F].
# Day 0 is 2275-01-01; years have no leap days. One focus runs at a time, and a focus's cost is its length in days [F].
#
# Justifying war goals [F]
#   Under allow_wargoals LIMITED a player needs no claim, no world tension and pays nothing extra.
#   take_state costs 200 + 50 per state of the target and drains 0.2 PP a day while it runs.
#   Each other war or justification running at the same time makes the next one 1.5x longer.
#   A target that is already at war counts as a major (OWB) and costs half.
#   Vanilla uses the same 200/50 costs, so expect roughly 2-4 months for a target of 1-4 states [M].
#   Check once on day 0: hover Justify on TRL and CCW. If it reads much over 90 days, push every war below back by the difference.
#
# Why rush [F]
#   Troll Warren (TRL) can fight no one before day 133 (its Wizard decision), and that first war can only be on the Carcass Walkers (CCW).
#   If MLT already owns CCW's 166 and 437 by then, TRL gets only a claim on MLT, and its mid-tree stays locked
#   (it needs TRL itself to annex CCW or MDT).
#   Left alone, TRL takes CCW (~2275-07), then RBT (from ~2275-09), then MDT (from ~2276-06), and only then turns on MLT.
#   So: CCW by ~day 125, TRL by ~day 270, RBT before MDT can make it a puppet (~2276-05). Nobody guarantees or allies any of them [F].
#
# Numbers used [F]
#   A military factory makes 2.0 IC a day at the 40 % efficiency cap: ~2.45 after scavtool, ~2.7 after specialised industry.
#   A mirelurk battalion is 375 IC, 30 manpower and 150 days of training.
#   A Coral Host (4 mirelurk + 5 infantry) is 1,750 IC and 245 manpower.
#   Mirelurk equipment needs 3 water per factory: MLT's 36 water runs 12 factories. The Wet Market sells equipment at 25 IC per cap.
#   Mirelurk armour is 15, and every tribal and super-mutant weapon in Oregon has piercing 1-2.
#   Mirelurk piercing is 7, and all of those units have armour 0.
#   The exception is TRL's one Woods Monsters division (4 behemoth, armour 20, piercing 36): the only unit that can hurt mirelurks.
#   Division armour is 0.4 x its best battalion + 0.6 x the average, so ONE mirelurk (armour 15) gives a militia division armour
#   ~6.75: still above every Oregon, tribal and Legion weapon. An anti-tank company (piercing 30 / 45) lifts an NCR-style division to
#   piercing ~15-22, which pierces that and the 8-mirelurk Tide Wall (15); from the NCR on, real armour (20+) matters [F defines, M].
#   Every marketplace sells at ~0.04 caps per IC (1 cap = 25 IC), in lots of 250, one lot per seller per 40 days [F].
#   MLT's own caps income is ~+12 a quarter (flat, never multiplied). Since round 17 the Kingdom makes M'lyeh a trade node: ~3.4 caps
#   a quarter at level 0, ~17 at level 3, the one income The Wet Market's +20 % multiplies. The Drowned Hoard (100 caps per 1,000
#   people every 60 days) is the big source [F formulas, M amounts].
#   Summoned divisions come fully equipped, with 20 % spare in the stockpile (32 Deep Ones, 16 Star Spawn) [F].

#   with the shared Oregon focuses as fillers while a conquest, egg, Book or population gate holds the next one.
#   One conquest block per target switches on when its stage comes - the previous target gone or at war, a focus, or a fallback
#   date - so the order survives a game that drifts: CCW from day 0, TRL, RBT, MDT, PMR and ARR after the Kingdom, the Oregon
#   ring, Cascadia, MXS / FOU / TDN, New Reno when it stands in the way, then the NCR and SHI, the interior, then Texas, Mexico

#   country of that side), and not behind an ally at half MLT's strength. The NCR's stage waits for the north (whoever holds
#   it), the NCR's cult at 40 % and New Reno out of the way (or 2284). Frontier conquests follow the north's land to whoever
#   took it and, after 90 days of asking for military access, take a country that cuts MLT off from an enemy. Once the NCR is

# Population is Rising Tide's currency. Rates:
#   the Drowned Hoard and the Wet Market turn ~2.5 IC of mirelurks out of each person;
#   a Deep Ones summon gives a trained, fully equipped division (+20 % spare) for 8,000 people; a Star Spawn summon one for 16,000;
#   the Grand Ritual takes 11.3 %, and the Final Ritual 36.3 % (180 days at 0.25 % a day).
#   For a gentler ~22 % over 180 days the Final Ritual's daily factor would be 0.0014.
# Water, not manpower, caps the creature army: 36 water runs 12 mirelurk `_1` factories, 7 on Deep Ones and 4 on Star Spawn,
#   so a trained Star Spawn division takes ~3 years. The Offering of the Tides (+4 water per 180 days) is the only renewable fix.
#   The Muttfruit branch's trades spend 24 of the capital's water.
# Research runs on 3 slots until When Shady Sands Falls (mltd.40 a: a fourth, ~2282) and The Last King Kneels (a fifth, ~2286).
#   Until then everything but creatures falls behind the NCR, the Brotherhoods and the Enclave; the summons, the Walk and the
#   permanent gifts carry MLT's late game, and the two late slots let industry catch up only once the great wars are decided.
# Cults (round 17): Found a Cult reaches any country the network covers, and Nurture the Cult is paid in equipment, not caps.
#   Two nurture operations hold a cult at ~76 % (100 % where an act's head shelters it). The Calls pay per point of total strength,
#   so several middling cults beat one strong one: three at 50 % pay 1,500 manpower, or 600 + 150 equipment, every 90 days.
#   The Spreading Cult still plants its free 20 % cults only in neighbouring majors (during a war, or on the NCR).
# Every story act's war focus declares its war itself (round 22), so from the NCR on MLT never justifies a major war: take it
#   only when the army is ready, because its completion is the declaration. Each act's close then waits for its great war to be
#   won - the NCR, the Legion, Texas: gone, capitulated, or its subject - so a war that stalls holds the story back.
# The early market matters: OWB's sellers supply ~4,000 infantry weapons a year from 2275 at 15 caps a lot, which is what equips
#   the ritual's 8,000 men as Shell Levies. From 2277 the Drowned Hoard's caps buy ~1,000 Wet Market mirelurks a year - water-free.
# Manpower is abundant from mid-2276 because MLT's units cost ~12.5 IC per man (a mirelurk battalion: 375 IC for 30 men; Deep Ones
#   ~17, Star Spawn 30) - equipment runs out long before men do - and armour halves the damage mirelurks take while each loses only
#   half a man per point of it (30 men on 60 HP). The egg ritual's +8,000 comes on top, then conquest and the Brood gift's +30 %.
#   So Indoctrinate the Faithful, the rituals' weekly manpower and Call for People add little; the Shell Levies are the one sink.
# The focus slot is saturated from the Kingdom to ~2280 only: Rising Tide's column is 670 days, of which the rituals hold 300,
#   and the 39 story focuses after the Walk are 1,559 days - 1,440 in one game (one ending; Hail the Drowned King or Drown the
#   Dance), 685 along the spine alone (one bead per fan and one ending). This plan takes 36 of them, the 1,440 days. But each
#   act after Act III opens only once the previous act's great war is won, so the slot idles through those wars - ~630 days of
#   the NCR's (after Act II's Washington branch), ~320 of the Legion's and ~340 of Texas's - on OWB's shared lurk_ focuses. A
#   real game takes every act; how late its finale comes is how fast it beats the NCR, the Legion and Texas.
# Decimation halves every conquered state's industry, so MLT's strength stays population and focus-granted units, never factories.
# Army size: fill half the combat width of every border province at peace (two 20-wide divisions an 80-wide tile) and three
#   quarters at war (three); past that, spend army experience on the templates and new equipment rather than on more
#   divisions. Fill it with the cheap line - Shell Levies and Coral Hosts on marketplace weapons (Butcher Pete's melee and the
#   Gun Runners' pipe guns and support equipment, 15-70 caps a lot) - and turn it into mirelurks only late. Run 2's AI, left to
#   the engine's factory-weighted count, held 402 divisions on 57 border states; run 3's, capped at two a border state, 51 on
#   100 border provinces.
