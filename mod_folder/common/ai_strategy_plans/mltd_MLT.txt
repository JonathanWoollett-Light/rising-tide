# Rising Tide - MLT AI strategy plans
#
# Three phase plans for an AI Mirelurk Tribe, in the order of CONQUEST_PLAN.txt: the Oregon opening until
# mlt_kingdom_of_mlyeh, the Kingdom and the Books until mltd_the_final_ritual, then the late game. Their enable
# and abort blocks are mutually exclusive, so one focus list runs at a time, and all three stop while MLT is a
# subject. A plan ends only through abort (an enable that turns false does not end it), so each abort is the
# next plan's enable; plans take no abort_when_not_enabled, which no vanilla or OWB plan uses.
#
# ai_national_focuses is taken in order, the first available entry first; unavailable entries are skipped, so
# the fillers at the end run while a conquest, egg, Book or population gate holds everything above them (OWB
# does the same at BDT.txt:43). focus_factors scales ai_will_do, which the AI consults only once no listed
# focus is available. research weights technology categories (common/technology_tags); ideas weights advisors
# by idea_token (common/characters/MLT.txt; OWB precedent BOS.txt:58-60).
