# The Leviathan: a capital ship with no module slots, so no ship part can be fitted, swapped or researched for it.
# Non-modular exactly as vanilla's repair ship is (common/units/equipment/repair_ships.txt: an archetype with no
# module_slots block, a variant with module_slots = inherit), so the designer offers nothing to customise and every
# stat below is the ship's own. It is never produced - can_be_produced is always false, so no dockyard can queue it
# even after mltd_leviathan_tech unlocks it - and is raised only by script: focus mltd_the_grand_ritual and the
# decision mltd_summon_the_leviathan (load_oob, as OWB spawns Broken Coast's Personal Floating Palace).
#
# Sized against OWB's Heavy Floating Fortress (ship_hull_heavy_5, 340 HP, detection 36, visibility 18, range 2000)
# fitted the way a late navy fits it - five ship_heavy_weapon_module_4, heavy_ship_armour_module_5, two hydrojets,
# heavy_ship_subsurface_module_3, detection, communications and command modules at level 5 - which works out at
# about 120 light / 280 heavy attack, 18 armour, 300-340 HP, 35 torpedo, speed ~21, visibility ~25 and range ~2,400.
# The Leviathan lands a little under it on firepower and a little over it on HP and armour, and differs where a
# thing of the deep should:
#   surface_visibility 8 (a third of the Fortress's): it is very hard to spot. Hit profile is visibility x the
#     sub-unit's hit_profile_mult (vanilla 00_defines.lua NNavy HIT_PROFILE_SPEED_FACTOR comment), so
#     mltd_leviathan's hit_profile_mult = 2.5 keeps it about as easy to hit as a Fortress once battle is joined -
#     hidden, not untouchable;
#   fuel_consumption 0: it burns no energy cells;
#   naval_speed 18: middling - a fitted Fortress makes ~21, a light hull 30 and more;
#   naval_range 6000: three times a Fortress's reach;
#   the weather: navy_weather_penalty is a country modifier, not a ship stat, so mltd_leviathan_tech carries it.
