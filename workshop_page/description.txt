[h1]OWB - Rising Tide[/h1]
A submod for [b]Old World Blues[/b] that gives the [b]Mirelurk Tribe[/b] somewhere to go once the Kingdom of M'lyeh is founded.
[h2]Small on purpose[/h2]
Adds [b]about a year of focuses[/b]. Every focus matters: no placeholder effects, no focuses that just hand you political power, no half-built branches.
[h2]What it adds[/h2]
Everything unlocks after Old World Blues' final Mirelurk focus, [i]The Kingdom of M'lyeh[/i].
[list]
[*] new units
[*] new focuses
[*] new decisions
[*] new leaders
[*] new ideas
[*] new operations
[*] new events
[*] new portraits
[*] new main menu
[/list]
[h2]Status[/h2]
Early release, and lightly tested. If something is broken or a number feels wrong, say so in the comments.
