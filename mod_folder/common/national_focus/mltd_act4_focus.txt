# Rising Tide (MLT) - Act IV, "The Drowned Republic" (round 27, the story acts).
# Shared focuses, pulled into mlt_nf through their prerequisites: the chapter, mltd_ncr_a_mole_in_shady_sands, hangs off
# Act III's close, mltd_the_tide_turns_south (mltd_act3_focus.txt), which the override lists (it hangs off the national
# Final Ritual); everything else here hangs off the chapter. No focus in this file is listed in the override.
#
# The act is the old NCR sub-tree (round 13) turned into one chapter of the spine (x = 15). Rows as of 2026-09-24, which
# put Act III's close at (15,28) (row 27 The Final Ritual, row 25 The Deep Ones Walk, each under an empty row, row 23 Act
# III; round 28c had the close on row 27 and every row below one higher); every cell here is relative, so it follows:
#   row 29  the chapter        A Mole in Shady Sands       (0,1) of mltd_the_tide_turns_south; the north's two spoils
#                              (mltd_spoils_focus.txt) flank it at (-2,0) and (2,0)
#   row 30  the fan            Drowned Delegates (-2,1), Sleepers on the Long 15 (0,1), Salt on the Caravan Roads (2,1)
#   row 31  the war            The Turbines Sing (0,2) - any one of the three beads opens it (one OR block)
#   row 32  the close          When Shady Sands Falls (0,3) - opens once the NCR has capitulated (now, to anyone, or to
#                              us in a war that has since ended: flag mltd_ncr_capitulated, set by on_capitulation), is
#                              gone, or is our subject; event mltd.40 (the trophy: a research slot, or the Drowned Rangers) and world
#                              news mltd.41
# Round 28c: Salt on the Caravan Roads now sits directly under a spoils focus (The Drowned Sound, 17,28), so its icon is
# OWB's GFX_goal_MOJ_ncr_trade (92x80, an NCR caravan) in place of GFX_goal_NCR_Caravan (149x154): a tall icon grows upward
# into the focus above it.
# Nothing was merged into this act: its five kept focuses keep their ids, costs, icons and rewards. What changed (rules in
# the round-27 build spec): the root is now the chapter (no Kingdom or NCR gate, ai_will_do 10); the three beads lost their
# intelligence gates (the intel rewards stay, inside has_dlc = "La Resistance") and pay a fallback when the NCR is gone;
# the war's prerequisite is any bead instead of the Sleepers alone; the round-23 is_ai modifiers that waited on The Deep Ones
# Walk are gone, because the tree now enforces the order. The war keeps its Hoover gate and its AI stage and readiness
# hold (mltd_ai_stage_ncr, mltd_ai_army_ready; since the round-27 review in `available`, mltd_ai_may_start_ncr_war), which
# now lifts once the focus can no longer start our war early - the NCR gone, our subject, at war with us, or capitulated to
# us (flag mltd_ncr_capitulated): the close needs the war focus, so an AI must not be held off it after our war has
# happened. A capitulation to anyone else does not lift it. Every focus is cancel_if_invalid = no + continue_if_invalid = yes.
#
# Round 28b (the play-test fixes):
# - Thefts are shares of the NCR's store, not flat lots: Salt on the Caravan Roads takes a fifth of its infantry and support
#   equipment, The Turbines Sing's rout a tenth of its rifles. 800 rifles was ~5 % of a year's arms output at the NCR's war
#   (~36 military factories, CONQUEST_PLAN.txt's 2279-12 snapshot) and less every year after; a share of the victim's own
#   reserve weighs the same in any year, on both sides of the war. Each share is read into a temp variable for the preview
#   (the tooltip prints it) and read again at completion; the transfer itself is hidden (CLAUDE.md > Theft without producer).
# - Round 28c (the review): the thefts pay in mirelurks, as Act V's and VI's do and as round 28b's spec asks from Act IV on.
#   The NCR still loses its rifles and crates, with no producer on the removal, but the haul is melted down into harness
#   and shell-plate for the brood - one mirelurk (the archetype amphibious_beast_equipment, our best unlocked breed) for
#   every five pieces taken, about their worth in IC. Rifles would still arm the plan's Shell Levies and Coral Hosts until
#   the NCR falls, but any factory makes rifles, while the creature lines are held to the water the tribe has
#   (CONQUEST_PLAN.txt's "mirelurk line 12 (36 water: the cap)") and the Tide Walls are bought mostly from the Wet Market:
#   mirelurks are what the tribe is short of in every act, and the levies and Coral Hosts field them too. A theft's
#   fallback is its floor: The Turbines Sing's rout pays 30 army experience whenever its haul comes to fewer than 30
#   mirelurks (Salt on the Caravan Roads has no fallback: its +100 caps come either way).
# - Every effect that hangs on a condition that can still change while the focus runs (the NCR's crisis, the Hoover outcome,
#   the second battle) is previewed as a condition with its native lines (effect_tooltip) and applied in hidden_effect, so the
#   tooltip is true whichever way the condition falls.
#
# Round 28c (readability): a reward that renders more than about six lines is broken into blocks with the house spacer,
# custom_effect_tooltip = mltd_newline_tt (loc " \n"; CLAUDE.md > The rituals, and the invitations in mltd_act3_focus.txt).
# The order of the blocks is what we gain, what the target loses, the intelligence, the war, then the event - never a
# reordering of the effects themselves, only blank lines between them. A spacer sits inside the branch it separates
# wherever the branch can fail, so it never prints for a branch that did not fire, and never last in a reward or a branch.
# Where a preview lists several outcomes of one condition ("if the battle has gone this way..."), each outcome is a block of
# its own: that is what tells the reader where one outcome ends. Presentation only - no number, gate or effect changed.
# Left alone, at four lines or fewer: the chapter, Sleepers on the Long 15 and Salt on the Caravan Roads.
#
# 2026-09-24 (the user: war focuses should bring bonuses against the country and an immediate buff, not stability and
# war-support losses of little consequence; and too much text): The Turbines Sing declares, steals its industry bonus and
# grants mltd_ncr_the_turbines_silenced (180 days, +10 % attack and defence against the NCR) and the shared 60-day
# mltd_the_first_wave (+10 % division recovery rate, +5 % breakthrough). The Hoover outcome's payouts (the Legion's rout, the
# held wall) and the second battle's -3 % war support, each previewed as a condition, are gone. Drowned Delegates lost its
# crisis line (-4 % more stability under ncr_crisis / ncr_emergency).

# The Esoteric Order opens its first eye in the Republic's capital
shared_focus = {
	id = mltd_ncr_a_mole_in_shady_sands
	icon = GFX_goal_NCR_Shady_Sands
	cost = 30
	prerequisite = { focus = mltd_the_tide_turns_south }
	# 2026-09-23: bypassed once NCR and MOT are all gone - with its nation gone it paid only its fallback
	bypass = {
		NOT = { country_exists = NCR }
		NOT = { country_exists = MOT }
	}
	cancel_if_invalid = no
	continue_if_invalid = yes
	x = 0
	y = 1
	relative_position_id = mltd_the_tide_turns_south
	ai_will_do = {
		factor = 10
	}
	completion_reward = {
		mltd_intel_foothold = yes
		# round 17: the cults of M'lyeh in the Republic and the Mojave wither half as fast (mltd_cult_refresh)
		if = {
			limit = {
				OR = {
					country_exists = NCR
					country_exists = MOT
				}
			}
			custom_effect_tooltip = mltd_ncr_cult_sheltered_tt
			hidden_effect = {
				if = {
					limit = { country_exists = NCR }
					NCR = { set_country_flag = mltd_cult_sheltered }
				}
				if = {
					limit = { country_exists = MOT }
					MOT = { set_country_flag = mltd_cult_sheltered }
				}
			}
		}
		if = {
			limit = {
				has_dlc = "La Resistance"
				country_exists = NCR
			}
			add_intel = {
				target = NCR
				civilian_intel = 10
				army_intel = 5
			}
		}
		else = {
			add_political_power = 25
		}
	}
}

shared_focus = {
	id = mltd_ncr_salt_on_the_caravan_roads
	icon = GFX_goal_MOJ_ncr_trade	# round 28c: a normal-height icon (see the header) - The Drowned Sound sits directly above
	cost = 30
	prerequisite = { focus = mltd_ncr_a_mole_in_shady_sands }
	# 2026-09-23: bypassed once NCR is gone - with its nation gone it paid only its fallback
	bypass = {
		NOT = { country_exists = NCR }
	}
	cancel_if_invalid = no
	continue_if_invalid = yes
	x = 2
	y = 1
	relative_position_id = mltd_ncr_a_mole_in_shady_sands
	ai_will_do = {
		factor = 3
	}
	completion_reward = {
		# theft without producer: the NCR loses the rifles whoever made them (CLAUDE.md > The story acts > Theft without
		# producer). Round 28b: a fifth of each store (it was 800 rifles, or 400, and 200 support), read now for the preview
		# and again at completion; only a store of at least 100 is worth a lost caravan. Round 28c (the review): we are paid in
		# mirelurks, as the Act V and VI thefts are (SPEC_R28B, rule 1: from Act IV on, pay in creature equipment). The
		# creature lines are what the tribe cannot build enough of - they are held to the water it has - while rifles come
		# from any factory; so the haul is melted down into harness and shell-plate for the brood, one mirelurk (the
		# archetype amphibious_beast_equipment, our best unlocked breed) for every five pieces taken, about their worth in IC.
		# No fallback: the +100 caps below come either way. The fifth and the five are literals here and in
		# mltd_ncr_caravan_theft_tt - change both.
		if = {
			limit = { country_exists = NCR }
			set_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_infantry = 0 }
			if = {
				limit = {
					NCR = { has_equipment = { infantry_equipment > 99 } }
				}
				set_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_infantry = NCR.num_equipment@infantry_equipment }
				multiply_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_infantry = 0.2 }
				round_temp_variable = mltd_theft_infantry
			}
			set_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_support = 0 }
			if = {
				limit = {
					NCR = { has_equipment = { support_equipment > 99 } }
				}
				set_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_support = NCR.num_equipment@support_equipment }
				multiply_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_support = 0.2 }
				round_temp_variable = mltd_theft_support
			}
			set_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_brood = mltd_theft_infantry }
			add_to_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_brood = mltd_theft_support }
			divide_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_brood = 5 }
			round_temp_variable = mltd_theft_brood
			custom_effect_tooltip = mltd_ncr_caravan_theft_tt
			hidden_effect = {
				if = {
					limit = {
						NCR = { has_equipment = { infantry_equipment > 99 } }
					}
					set_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_negative = mltd_theft_infantry }
					multiply_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_negative = -1 }
					NCR = {
						add_equipment_to_stockpile = {
							type = infantry_equipment
							amount = mltd_theft_negative
						}
					}
				}
				if = {
					limit = {
						NCR = { has_equipment = { support_equipment > 99 } }
					}
					set_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_negative = mltd_theft_support }
					multiply_temp_variable = { mltd_theft_negative = -1 }
					NCR = {
						add_equipment_to_stockpile = {
							type = support_equipment
							amount = mltd_theft_negative
						}
					}
				}
				if = {
					limit = { check_variable = { mltd_theft_brood > 0 } }
					add_equipment_to_stockpile = {
						type = amphibious_beast_equipment
						amount = mltd_theft_brood
					}
				}
			}
		}
		# round 27: the NCR gone, there are no caravans left to lose
		else = {
			add_political_power = 25
		}
		set_temp_variable = { caps_to_add = 100 }
		add_caps = yes
	}
}

shared_focus = {
	id = mltd_ncr_the_turbines_sing
	icon = GFX_goal_generic_dam
	cost = 30
	# round 27: any bead of the fan opens the war (one OR block); it used to hang off the Sleepers alone
	prerequisite = {
		focus = mltd_ncr_drowned_delegates
		focus = mltd_ncr_sleepers_on_the_long_15
		focus = mltd_ncr_salt_on_the_caravan_roads
	}
	available = {
		custom_trigger_tooltip = {
			tooltip = mltd_ncr_hoover_contested_tt
			OR = {
				has_global_flag = fbhd_legion_overall_vic
				has_global_flag = fbhd_boulder_city_miracle_flag
				has_global_flag = fbhd_mot_hold_dam
				NOT = { country_exists = CES }
				AND = {
					country_exists = CES
					CES = {
						OR = {
							has_completed_focus = ces_hoover_dam_battle
							has_completed_focus = ces_attack_ncr
						}
					}
				}
				AND = {
					country_exists = NCR
					NCR = { has_war_with = CES }
				}
				date > 2279.06.01
			}
		}
		# round 22: the focus is the declaration, so an AI takes it only when its stage has come and the army is ready. Round
		# 27: the war is now a node of the spine, and the close needs it, so the hold lifts once there is no war left to start
		# early - the NCR already at war with us (the conquest block got there first), gone, our subject or capitulated to us
		# (not to anyone else: a capitulated NCR whose allies fight on would still be declared on). Since the round-27 review
		# the hold sits here, hidden and AI-only, because an AI takes any listed focus that is available whatever its
		# ai_will_do reads (mltd_ai_triggers.txt, section W)
		mltd_ai_may_start_ncr_war = yes
	}
	# 2026-09-23: bypassed once NCR is gone - with its nation gone it paid only its fallback
	bypass = {
		NOT = { country_exists = NCR }
	}
	cancel_if_invalid = no
	continue_if_invalid = yes
	x = 0
	y = 2
	relative_position_id = mltd_ncr_a_mole_in_shady_sands
	ai_will_do = {
		factor = 3
	}
	will_lead_to_war_with = NCR
	completion_reward = {
		# round 22: the branch ends in the war itself - the focus declares on each living target that is not already at war
		# with us or our subject, so its own length is the run-up and will_lead_to_war_with warns them

			limit = {
				country_exists = NCR
				NOT = { has_war_with = NCR }
				NCR = { NOT = { is_subject_of = ROOT } }
			}
			declare_war_on = {
				type = annex_everything
				target = NCR
			}
		}
		# 2026-09-24 (the user: war focuses should bring bonuses against the country and an immediate buff, not stability and
		# war-support losses of little consequence; and less text): the war, then - against a target that is not MLT's subject -
		# the 180-day idea with +10 % attack and defence against it, The First Wave (60 days) and a stolen industry bonus; with the
		# target MLT's subject, +50 political power. The side branches keyed to the target's own story went with the losses
		# (the first Battle for Hoover Dam's rout and wall, previewed with every outcome while undecided, and the second battle's
		# war-support loss: round 28b-2026-09-23)
		if = {
			limit = {
				country_exists = NCR
				NCR = { NOT = { is_subject_of = ROOT } }
			}
			add_timed_idea = {
				idea = mltd_ncr_the_turbines_silenced
				days = 180
			}
			add_timed_idea = {
				idea = mltd_the_first_wave
				days = 60
			}
		}
		else = {
			add_political_power = 50
		}
		if = {
			limit = {
				has_dlc = "La Resistance"
				country_exists = NCR
			}
			steal_random_tech_bonus = {
				folder = fallout_industry_folder
				ahead_reduction = 1
				bonus = 1.0
				base_bonus = 0.5
				name = mltd_ncr_the_turbines_sing
				target = NCR
				uses = 1
			}
		}
		else = {
			add_tech_bonus = {
				name = mltd_ncr_the_turbines_sing
				bonus = 0.5
				uses = 1
				category = industry
			}
		}
	}

############ Act IV - the close ############

# The Republic is broken: the trophy (event mltd.40 - a research slot, or the Drowned Rangers and 50 army experience) and the
# world news (mltd.41). mltd_ai_ncr_beaten (common/scripted_triggers/mltd_ai_triggers.txt) is the NCR gone or capitulated
# now. has_capitulated clears when the country leaves its last war, so an NCR that survives the peace as a rump (a human
# who takes only some states, or allies who share the peace) would fail it for good although we won; the country flag
# mltd_ncr_capitulated, set on MLT by on_capitulation (common/on_actions/mltd_on_actions.txt) when the NCR capitulates while
# at war with MLT, keeps the win. A subject NCR counts as beaten too: a human who puppets it at the peace would otherwise
# wait for ever.
shared_focus = {
	id = mltd_when_shady_sands_falls
	icon = GFX_goal_NCR_FO2_Tattered_Flag
	cost = 30
	prerequisite = { focus = mltd_ncr_the_turbines_sing }
	available = {
		custom_trigger_tooltip = {
			tooltip = mltd_ncr_beaten_tt
			OR = {
				mltd_ai_ncr_beaten = yes
				has_country_flag = mltd_ncr_capitulated
				NCR = { is_subject_of = ROOT }
			}
		}
	}
	cancel_if_invalid = no
	continue_if_invalid = yes
	x = 0
	y = 3
	relative_position_id = mltd_ncr_a_mole_in_shady_sands
	ai_will_do = {
		factor = 10
	}
	completion_reward = {
		# the event's two options, previewed with their own native lines (effect_tooltip shows and does not execute): the same
		# effects as mltd.40's options a and b (events/mltd_events.txt) - change both together
		custom_effect_tooltip = mltd_shady_sands_falls_choice_tt
		custom_effect_tooltip = mltd_shady_sands_falls_scholars_tt
		effect_tooltip = {
			add_research_slot = 1
		}
		# round 28c: a block for each of the trophy's two options
		custom_effect_tooltip = mltd_newline_tt
		custom_effect_tooltip = mltd_shady_sands_falls_rangers_tt
		effect_tooltip = {
			add_ideas = mltd_the_drowned_rangers
			army_experience = 100
		}
		custom_effect_tooltip = mltd_newline_tt
		country_event = { id = mltd.40 days = 1 }
		hidden_effect = {
			every_other_country = {
				news_event = { id = mltd.41 days = 2 }
			}
		}
	}
}
